The Abundance Clock

A counter to the Doomsday Clock

Since 1947, the Bulletin of the Atomic Scientists has told us how close we are to midnight — to destruction. Their clock currently reads 89 seconds to midnight. But the world hasn't ended. What has happened is the greatest explosion of prosperity, health, and technological capability in human history.

So we built a different clock. The Abundance Clock measures how far we've come from midnight — how far into the dawn we are. Each technology on this dashboard pushes the clock further from darkness and deeper into light.

METHODOLOGY

How We Track Progress

📊

Real Data, Not Hype

Every metric on this dashboard is backed by a measurable time series — costs, deployments, performance benchmarks, or production volumes. We track what can be plotted on a log scale.

🎯

Tipping Points

For each technology, we define a "tipping point" — the threshold where adoption goes from linear to exponential. Progress bars show how far each technology has traveled toward this threshold.

🚀

Acceleration Score

A 1-5 score measuring whether progress is decelerating, steady, or accelerating. Based on the rate of change of the rate of change — the second derivative. A score of 5 means the curve is bending vertical.

🔗

Compound Effects

Technologies don't exist in isolation. Cheaper AI makes drug discovery faster. Cheaper energy makes compute cheaper. Cheaper compute makes AI better. We track these compounding loops.
